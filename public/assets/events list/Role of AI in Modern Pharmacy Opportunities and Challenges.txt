Our students had the opportunity to attend the National Conference – Pharmacon 2026, themed “Role of AI in Modern Pharmacy: Opportunities & Challenges,” held on 11 September 2026 at Ravindra Bhavan, Bhopal. The conference was organized by Arogya Bharati in collaboration with the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and the State Pharmacy Council.
The conference brought together distinguished personalities, eminent pharmacy leaders, experts, academicians, and renowned speakers from the fields of pharmacy and healthcare. The speakers shared valuable insights into the importance of pharmacy and its evolving role in modern healthcare.
A key highlight of the event was the address by the President of the Pharmacy Council, who spoke extensively about the growing role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in pharmacy and its potential to transform pharmaceutical research, drug development, healthcare services, and professional practice. The importance of pharmacists in ensuring safe, effective, and patient-centered healthcare was also strongly emphasized. 💊

The engaging Panel Discussion further explored the challenges and opportunities associated with AI in pharmacy, emerging career prospects, technological advancements, and the skills required for future pharmacy professionals.

Our students actively participated in the conference and enthusiastically took part in Poster Presentations and various other competitions, showcasing their creativity, research aptitude, subject knowledge, confidence, and innovative thinking. 🎓🏆

The insightful lectures, expert interactions, and thought-provoking discussions made the event a highly informative and fruitful learning experience for our students. The conference provided them with a broader perspective on the future of pharmacy, the importance of pharmacists in healthcare, and the transformative potential of AI in the profession.
