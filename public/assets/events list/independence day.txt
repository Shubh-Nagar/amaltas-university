Title :  Independence Day Celebration 2026 | Amaltas University 


Content :  The Independence Day Celebration on 15th August was celebrated at Amaltas University with great enthusiasm, patriotic spirit, and pride. The program commenced with the flag hoisting ceremony by Mr. Mayank Raj Singh Bhadoria, Chairman, Amaltas Group, who extended heartfelt Independence Day wishes to everyone.

The Chief Guests, Mr. Rajesh Rathore, Executive Director, MPIDC, and Mr. Dileep Jat, Sarpanch of Village Bangar, addressed the gathering with inspiring words. They encouraged the students to contribute meaningfully to nation-building and wished them a bright and successful future.

The event was graced by senior university officials, principals, doctors, staff members, students, and members of the Amaltas family. The patriotic songs, dances, and poetry performances by the students filled the entire campus with a strong sense of patriotism, pride, and enthusiasm.

The program concluded with the National Anthem. The celebration further strengthened the values of unity, respect, patriotism, and love for the nation among everyone present.


Date : 15 Aug 2026
