Amaltas Institute of Medical Sciences (Paramedical Sciences) conducted a clinical teaching session for pathology students in the Department of Microbiology on *13 August 2026 at 3:00 PM onwards. The session was conducted by **Mr. Suraj Sharma* on *“Culturing and Staining Process.”

Students learned about the basic principles of microbial culture, culture media, aseptic techniques, and staining methods, with special emphasis on *Gram staining* and its clinical importance. The session was interactive and helped students strengthen their practical understanding of microbiological laboratory procedures.

The programme concluded successfully, with active participation and valuable learning outcomes for the students.
